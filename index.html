The LAP promise, in full

Your return window starts at delivery — not after it.

Every other platform makes you decide before you've touched the product, then punishes you with a week-long return process when it's wrong. LAP flips that: the trial happens live, at your door, and the decision is instant.

The usual way

  • Decide from a photo — often filtered or over-lit
  • Item arrives days later
  • Doesn't fit or doesn't match → raise a return request
  • Wait for pickup to be "assigned"
  • Wait 3–7+ days for the pickup itself
  • Refund processes after the warehouse checks it

LAP's way

  • Decide from real, unfiltered photos + fit notes
  • Item arrives in ~10 minutes, where serviceable
  • Try it on with the rider waiting at your door
  • Keep it, or hand it back on the spot
  • No pickup request. No "agent assigned." No waiting.
  • Refund initiated the moment it's handed back
