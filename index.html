CONCEPT PREVIEW

An honest stylist, not a search bar.

Tell it what you own, your budget, your body type, the occasion. It tells you exactly what to add next — and why — instead of burying you under ten thousand results that all look the same.

  • Built around what's already in your wardrobe
  • Explains the "why," not just the "buy"
  • No sponsored placements disguised as suggestions

I have a wedding reception, budget's ₹4,000, I mostly wear black

Go for a sage-green kurta set — it breaks your black streak without fighting it, and it photographs better under warm reception lighting than black does. Pair with a darker mojari, not black shoes.

Why not black shoes?

Black shoes with sage-green flatten the outfit in photos. A brown or oxblood mojari gives it contrast without clashing.
